Function staleness_weight 

Source 
pub fn staleness_weight(
    age: Duration,
    sync_interval: Duration,
    stale_timeout: Duration,
) -> f64
Expand description

Compute the staleness weight for an observation of the given age.

  • Full weight (1.0) for ages up to 2 × sync_interval — a healthy peer publishes every sync_interval, so anything fresher than two intervals is just normal propagation delay, not staleness
  • Linear decay from 1.0 to 0.0 between 2 × sync_interval and stale_timeout
  • Zero at and beyond stale_timeout — the peer is presumed dead or partitioned and its last known rate is phantom load

Degenerate configurations (stale_timeout <= 2 × sync_interval) collapse to a hard cutoff at stale_timeout.

Lives in the engine module (always compiled) because both the gossip aggregation layer and the PidEngine liveness test use it.
